02

Install once. Keep your settings.

The Windows installer adds the local updater. Future releases appear in the extension’s version section, with release notes, skip and rollback controls.

Run the Windows installer

Download the .exe above and choose Install / Repair (安装 / 修复). The updater only runs when needed; it does not stay resident.

Enable Developer mode

The installer opens the browser’s extensions page and copies the extension folder path. Turn on Developer mode.

Load the unpacked extension

Choose Load unpacked, paste the copied path with Ctrl+V, and select the Extension folder. Pin the extension icon for easy access.

Prefer manual installation?

Extract the ZIP, open chrome://extensions or edge://extensions, enable Developer mode, and load the extracted folder. ZIP-only installation does not include the native one-click updater.

Current installer file: 985gmgn-helper-setup-v0.46.111.exe

03

Connect only what you need.

Most tools have separate switches. Similar-token panels, strategies and audio alerts are opt-in.

Open GMGN or DeBot

Use a tracking page for mixed activity, or a token page for the FOMO panel. Aggregate Monitor and trench enhancements are GMGN-specific.

Connect your 985monitor account

Sign in to 985monitor in the same browser profile to connect FOMO/Pump feeds and your account filters. If that session becomes invalid, the feeds pause rather than falling back to a public feed.

Sign in to FOMO for its panel

For FOMO holders and theses, sign in to fomo.family with your own account. The panel provides a sign-in guide when renewal is no longer possible.

04

Clear data boundaries.

No private keys or seed phrases are requested. Alerts and overlays do not place trades for you.

Local extension settingsExtension watchlists, colors, strategy rules and alert history stay in browser storage. 985monitor account preferences remain with that service, with a local cache in the extension.
FOMO sessionFOMO credentials are used for FOMO authentication and supported renewal. They are not uploaded to 985monitor. A separate read-only 985monitor session supplies filtered feeds.
Public leaderboard contributionAfter accepting the update notice, eligible online users may be selected to submit public FOMO leaderboard data to 985monitor. Tokens, cookies, private trades and personal follow lists are excluded.
Local translationThesis translation uses the browser’s built-in model where supported. Original text remains visible; the website language switch is independent.

05

A few practical answers.

Installation, connection and feature availability.

Which features work on DeBot?
DeBot supports FOMO/Pump tracking cards, the FOMO panel, similar-token panels, priority-person alerts and Brew. Aggregate Monitor, GMGN follow-control shortcuts and trench fee badges are GMGN-specific.
Why are no FOMO/Pump cards appearing?
Open GMGN tracking or DeBot tracking records and enable the relevant feed in the extension. Check your 985monitor connection and account filters; a missing or expired session stops the feed.
Does FOMO stay signed in forever?
No. The extension attempts supported renewal, but FOMO can expire or revoke a session. Follow the panel’s login guide when asked; repeatedly retrying will not bypass rate limits.
Are aggregate totals the whole market?
No. They summarize activity received by the current page within your selected window. Buyer identities are deduplicated where verifiable. Unknown USD amounts or full-exit status are not invented.
Will upgrading remove my settings?
The official extension keeps a fixed ID so browser settings remain in place. The installer backs up runtime files before an update, and offers rollback for supported official versions.
Do fee checkmarks mean a token is safe?
No. A Genius checkmark only indicates that the supported fee data is at or below the 0.25% Creator payout threshold. It is not a contract audit, endorsement or prediction of returns.
